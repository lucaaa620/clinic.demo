About the Clinic

Expert Care, Compassionate Heart

Dr. K.S. Bisoi's Vitality Care Clinic is a dedicated Metabolic Clinic serving the people of Angul, Odisha. Located near Bus Stand at Kalia Sahu Market Complex, we offer expert consultation and treatment for a wide range of general medicine and metabolic health conditions.
